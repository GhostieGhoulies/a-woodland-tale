Privacy Policy for A Forest Tale

Effective Date: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how A Forest Tale ("the App") handles user information. We respect your privacy and designed the App to be played without collecting personal data.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information.

  • No user accounts, logins, names, or email addresses are required or collected.
  • No location data, contacts, photos, or device identifiers are accessed or transmitted.
  • Any local game progress (such as high scores) is stored strictly on your own device and is never sent to external servers.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The App does not contain third-party advertising networks, analytics trackers, or in-app purchases that collect user data.

3. Children's Privacy

Because the App does not collect any personal information from anyone, it complies with children's privacy standards, including COPPA. Users of all ages can play safely without submitting personal data.

4. Changes to This Policy

If future updates to the App introduce features that change how data is handled, this Privacy Policy will be updated on this page with a revised effective date.
